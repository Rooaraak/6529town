How to become a validator

  1. 1Hold a Meme card

    You need at least one card from The Memes by 6529. Your cards give you TDH, and TDH is what a validator signs for.

    The Memes on OpenSea ↗

  2. 2Download the validator

    Only from 6529.io/validator. It runs on Windows, Mac and Linux.

    Get the validator ↗

  3. 3Install it and sign once

    The app makes a key for your computer and asks your wallet to sign one message. It costs no gas and moves nothing.

  4. 4Keep the computer on

    The validator signs once a day by itself. Your first signature is the next day, and then your house stands in the town.

Never share your seed phrase. Always check what you sign with your wallet.

    Kind of place

    Unofficial community project, not made by 6529. Numbers update every 2 hours.

    How it works
    • Numbers. Read from one validator's own Network page every 2 hours. A day is final at 19:30 UTC. Per house the page keeps a few facts (first day, part of the world, data center, took part, signed) and, for “Find your house”, a scrambled wallet code with that wallet's TDH, which only shows when someone enters that wallet's address. No wallets, no names.
    • Bell. Only on the house you found and kept. If you turn it on, the town stores this device's notification address and your house number. No wallet, no name. It rings when your house has not signed about two hours before signing closes, and when a new validator version is out. Turning it off or moving out deletes it.
    • Houses. One per validator. Each continent has its own quarter; the lot inside it is made up. Roof colour shows the continent reported that day. Data centers stand apart, beyond the factory. Places are what each validator chose to publish. A garden tree grows a little every day for its first weeks, and a lantern marks the houses here since the first day of house records.
    • Planes and lights. Paper planes fly between houses that took part that day. Gold lights run from houses that signed to the Ministry of Validation. Both are for show, not one per real message.
    • Sound. Off until you tap the speaker. Eight quiet songs: the town's own tune, then free music (CC0) by cynicmusic, Destin715, KarateStudios, HoliznaCC0 and Tsorthan Grove. On a computer, a soft chime is a gold light being counted.
    • First days. Wed 4 to Sat 7 Nov ran on the network's old chain, which kept only each day's totals, so the houses on those days stand in for the real ones.
    • Relays. From punk6529's announcement.
    • Art. The frog and the citizen are original characters. Meme Cards are CC0; tap the Meme Museum in town to see the card on show and its artist.
